# Brag Plan: Beemo Studio Redesign

## The 9-question rubric
1. **What is the app?**
   A redesign of the Beemo Studio website from its original design to a modern, cinematic audiovisual production portfolio.
2. **What is the funniest or most impressive claim?**
   The stark contrast between the old site and the new dark-mode cinematic experience.
3. **What is the visual hook?**
   The transition from the old `before-capture` screenshots to the new `after-capture` screenshots, showing the huge upgrade in design quality.
4. **What should be shown from the actual UI?**
   The old hero section vs the new hero section, and the old portfolio vs the new horizontal scrolling cards.
5. **What is the shortest satisfying video?**
   ~15 seconds.
6. **What tone fits best?**
   Preset: cinematic. Direction: premium redesign reveal.
7. **What should the audio feel like?**
   Cinematic support. Suspenseful build up during the "Before" leading to a massive drop for the "After".
8. **What should the share caption say?**
   Just redesigned Beemo Studio. From static to cinematic. What do you think of the new look?
9. **What's the user flow worth showing?**
   Hero Section Before -> Hero Section After.

## Storyboard
1. **The Before (0-3s)**: Show the screenshot of the old site's hero section (`before-capture/screenshots/scroll-000.png`). A red "BEFORE" label sits in the corner.
2. **The Wipe (3-4.5s)**: A clean, sharp wipe reveals the new site's hero section (`after-capture/screenshots/scroll-000.png`). A green "AFTER" label appears.
3. **The After - Hero (4.5-8s)**: Slowly zoom in on the new hero section.
4. **The After - Portfolio (8-13s)**: Show the new portfolio section (`after-capture/screenshots/scroll-028.png` or similar) to highlight the new UI.
5. **Outro (13-15s)**: Text: "Redesigned by you."
