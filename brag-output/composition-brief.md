# Hyperframes Composition Brief: Beemo Studio Redesign

## Objective
Create a before-and-after redesign showcase video comparing the old live site to the new Beemo Studio design.

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output/comparison-composition/`
- Rendered video: `brag-output/comparison.mp4`
- Format: landscape — 1920x1080
- Duration: 15 seconds

## Source Material
- Old site capture: `before-capture/screenshots/scroll-000.png`
- New site capture: `after-capture/screenshots/scroll-000.png` and `after-capture/screenshots/scroll-015.png`

## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: premium redesign reveal
- Angle: From a static, dated design to a high-end, immersive cinematic portfolio.
- Hook: The sharp wipe transition from the old screenshot to the new design.

## Visual Identity
- Background: `#050505`
- Text: `#ffffff`
- Display font: `Syncopate`
- Body font: `Plus Jakarta Sans`

## Storyboard
1. The Before (0-3s): Show the old site hero screenshot full screen.
2. The Wipe (3-4.5s): A diagonal wipe reveals the new site hero screenshot.
3. The After - Hero (4.5-8s): Zoom in slowly on the new hero.
4. The After - Portfolio (8-13s): Cut to the new portfolio screenshot (`scroll-015.png`).
5. Outro (13-15s): Text: "Redesigned by you."
